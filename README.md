Dip-hunter: a slow mean-reversion bot trading Coinbase Wrapped BTC (cbBTC) against USDC on one-minute bars. It buys deep dips below the 300-bar Bollinger lower band while the 14-bar RSI is under 35, and sells at +1.5% over its cost or −1% under it. Dev-window net PnL +$13.34 on $1,000 of paper money, 3 trades, max drawdown 0.78%.

## Run it

```sh
node main.ts
```
